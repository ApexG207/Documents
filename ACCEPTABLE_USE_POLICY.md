# MatIQ Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026

Users of MatIQ must use the service lawfully, safely, and consistently with athlete welfare, privacy, academy authority, and competition rules.

Prohibited uses include unauthorized access; credential sharing; bypassing role, tenant, guardian, privacy, consent, retention, audit, or security controls; harassment, exploitation, grooming, stalking, or abuse; unlawful surveillance; non-consensual media collection; submission of content without required rights or consent; scraping, harvesting, or bulk extraction; malware, denial-of-service, or security testing without authorization; deceptive identity or academy claims; falsification of competition or athlete evidence; use of MatIQ to discriminate unlawfully; and use of MatIQ or its non-public materials to create or train a competing commercial product without written permission.

MatIQ must not be used as the sole basis for medical, injury, return-to-play, weight-management, safeguarding, promotion, disciplinary, or other consequential decisions. Qualified human judgment remains required.

Where a minor or protected participant is involved, age-aware restrictions, guardian consent, media permissions, and safeguarding controls remain mandatory and cannot be overridden by administrative authority.

Apex Governance Group may investigate suspected abuse, preserve relevant audit evidence, restrict functionality, suspend access, or terminate service where reasonably necessary to protect users, athletes, customers, the platform, or legal obligations.

This policy supplements the MatIQ Terms of Service, EULA, Proprietary Commercial Software License, Privacy Policy, and applicable customer agreements.
