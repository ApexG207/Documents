# Privacy and Youth-Safety Control Framework

## Governing posture

MatIQ is designed for adult-controlled use on behalf of youth athletes. It does not permit unsupervised child accounts or direct child-to-AI conversations in the pilot.

## Required controls

- Verifiable parental consent before collecting child personal information when required.
- Plain-language notice covering categories, purposes, sharing, retention, access, correction, export, and deletion.
- Data minimization: collect only what supports development, safety, event administration, and approved evaluation.
- Purpose limitation for media; no public publishing by default.
- Consent withdrawal without retaliation or loss of unrelated academy services.
- Bounded retention with deletion holds only for documented legal or safety needs.
- Human review of AI output; no diagnosis, treatment, nutrition prescription, dangerous weight manipulation, or outcome guarantees.
- Incident escalation to the product owner, privacy lead, safeguarding lead, affected academy, and counsel as applicable.

## Prohibited data and uses

- Government identifiers, financial data, precise home addresses, or unnecessary health records.
- Facial recognition, biometric identification, emotion inference, or covert profiling.
- Advertising profiles, sale of child information, or cross-context behavioral advertising.
- Public athlete rankings or searchable child profiles.
- Automated eligibility, discipline, medical, or safeguarding decisions.

## Rights workflow

Requests are logged, identity and authority are verified, scope is determined, records are preserved from unrelated change, the response is approved, and completion evidence is retained. Production service targets will be set by counsel for applicable jurisdiction.

This framework is an operational draft and must be reviewed by qualified counsel before public use.
