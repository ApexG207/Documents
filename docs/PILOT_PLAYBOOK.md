# Academy Design-Partner Pilot Playbook

## Mission

Validate that MatIQ improves coaching decisions and institutional retention while protecting youth athletes and reducing administrative burden.

## Cohort

One to two academies, 10–30 athletes each, 60–90 days, controlled Gi and no-Gi workflows, no public data, and named academy administrators.

## Commercial construct

Recommended design-partner fee: $149–$299 per academy per month. Pilot scope, limits, support, data responsibilities, exit rights, confidentiality, and feedback use are documented in a signed agreement.

## Onboarding sequence

1. Execute academy agreement and data-processing terms.
2. Confirm administrator and safeguarding point of contact.
3. Import no records until parent notices and consent are complete.
4. Train coaches on evidence standards, AI limits, and incident reporting.
5. Establish baseline effort, record quality, and competition-preparation measures.
6. Conduct weekly feedback and defect triage.
7. Conduct midpoint risk and value review.
8. Complete final outcomes and renewal decision.

## Stop conditions

Pause collection or use for unauthorized access, consent failure, material safeguarding concern, critical security defect, data mixing between academies, or AI behavior that cannot be reliably governed.
