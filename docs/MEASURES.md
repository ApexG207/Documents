# MatIQ Measurement and Assessment Plan

| Type | Measure | Definition | Pilot target | Owner |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| MOP | Coach activation | Invited coaches completing onboarding | 90% | Academy admin |
| MOP | Record coverage | Active athletes with weekly evidence | 60% | Coach lead |
| MOP | Plan completion | Competition plans completed by gate | 80% | Coach lead |
| MOE | Admin-time reduction | Change from baseline weekly admin time | 30% reduction | Product manager |
| MOE | Decision latency | Time from match to approved intervention | 72 hours or less | Coach lead |
| MOE | Renewal intent | Academies indicating paid continuation | 80% | Product owner |
| KPI | Consent compliance | Required consents valid before processing | 100% | Privacy lead |
| KPI | Unauthorized disclosure | Confirmed unauthorized data events | 0 | Technical lead |
| KPI | Critical defects | Open severity-one defects at release | 0 | Technical lead |
| KPI | AI acceptance | Outputs approved with minor or no edits | 70% | Coach lead |

Measures are reviewed weekly during the pilot and at formal midpoint and closeout assessments. Metrics must not incentivize unsafe training, weight manipulation, child comparison, or unnecessary data collection.
