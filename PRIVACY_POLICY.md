# MatIQ Privacy Policy — Repository Baseline

Effective date: 25 August 2026

This repository baseline describes the privacy principles MatIQ is designed to support. The production privacy notice presented to users must be reviewed for the actual deployment, jurisdictions, service providers, data flows, retention schedules, and customer agreements in effect at launch.

## Scope

MatIQ may process account information, athlete profiles, academy information, training records, competition records, skill and promotion evidence, media, verification records, guardian-consent records, support and moderation information, payment-related identifiers, audit events, device or service telemetry, and other information users choose to provide.

## Purposes

Information may be processed to provide and secure MatIQ; authenticate users; enforce roles and academy boundaries; support training, coaching, competition preparation, athlete development, verification, media handling, payments, backups, retention, safety, moderation, analytics, support, legal compliance, and product improvement consistent with applicable agreements and law.

## Minors and protected participants

MatIQ uses age-aware controls. Where a participant is a minor, guardian consent, restricted permissions, media controls, and other protective measures may be required. Administrative, academy, coach, founder, or governance authority does not waive those protections.

## Data minimization and access

MatIQ should collect and retain only information reasonably necessary for authorized product functions and governed records. Access should be limited by identity, role, academy context, consent, and operational need.

## Media and sensitive content

Users must have lawful authority and any required consent before uploading video, images, documents, or other media. Media access, retention, export, and deletion controls must respect applicable athlete, guardian, academy, event, and legal requirements.

## Service providers

MatIQ may rely on cloud infrastructure, identity, payment, email, AI, analytics, app-store, and other service providers. Production notices should identify material provider categories and any disclosures required by applicable law.

## Data rights

Subject to applicable law and contractual roles, users may have rights to access, correct, export, delete, restrict, object to, or otherwise control personal information. MatIQ should provide governed workflows for applicable privacy requests and account deletion.

## Security

MatIQ uses role-based authorization, tenant-aware controls, audit logging, integrity checks, encryption where appropriate, security testing, backups, retention controls, and incident-response processes. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention

Personal information should be retained only as long as required for authorized product purposes, contractual commitments, legal obligations, safety, dispute resolution, or legitimate archival requirements. Legal holds and mandatory retention requirements may delay deletion where lawful.

## AI and automated assistance

AI-assisted features are intended to organize or analyze information in support of human decision-making. MatIQ should not use AI output as the sole basis for medical, safeguarding, promotion, disciplinary, or similarly consequential decisions.

## Sale and targeted advertising

MatIQ is not designed around the sale of athlete personal information. Any production use of advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, data brokerage, or materially different secondary use requires explicit policy review and any consent or opt-out rights required by law.

## Changes and contact

The production Privacy Policy should provide the current privacy contact, effective date, material-change process, and jurisdiction-specific disclosures before general release.

This document is an operational privacy baseline and not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.
